Privacy Policy for Neon Dash Orbit Bounce

Neon Dash Orbit Bounce is developed as a free/arcade game. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. All game scores, lives, and skin selections are stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services:
- Google AdMob is used to display rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad delivery.
- Google Play Billing is used for secure in-app purchases. Purchase transactions are handled securely by Google Play.

Data Deletion:
Since no personal user data is stored on external servers by this app, no account deletion is necessary. Users wishing to clear their local game data can clear the app's cache and storage directly from their Android device settings at any time.
